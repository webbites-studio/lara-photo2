About

Lara Nikolau

A Boston photographer drawn to genuine emotion, quiet light, and the unarranged moment.

Lara photographs families, couples, maternity, weddings, and individuals across Boston, Acton, and Massachusetts — with destination work by request. Her practice is built on patience: long, unhurried hours observing light and gesture before a frame is made.

Originally trained in fine art, she brings a painter’s sense of tone and restraint to every sitting — favoring natural light, honest expression, and a muted, timeless palette over trend.

Philosophy

Light first. Direction quiet. Editing restrained. The photograph should feel discovered, not constructed.

Experience

More than a decade making portraits and documenting weddings across New England and beyond.

Specialties

Family · Couples · Maternity · Wedding · Individual · Fashion & editorial

Location

Based in Boston & Acton, Massachusetts. Booking 2026 worldwide travel.
